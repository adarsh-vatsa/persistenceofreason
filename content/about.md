## On this notebook

Most of what I write here is unfinished on purpose. Each piece carries a status that says how
far along the thinking is.

- *Conjecture.* A hunch, possibly wrong, written down so I can find out.
- *Sketch.* The argument is taking shape, with gaps I know about.
- *Settled.* I would defend it, until someone shows me otherwise.

If a piece turns out to be wrong, I say so in a later piece rather than quietly editing the old one.

about

I'm a PhD student in computer science at the Stevens Institute of Technology, working with William Eiers. My research sits where language models meet formal verification. I build systems that turn natural-language access-control requirements into policies a symbolic checker can actually prove correct, then use that checker's feedback to train the models.A policy that reads right and a policy that is right are surprisingly different things. Most of my work lives in that gap.

Outside of that, I like math, deep learning, and ideas that are not quite finished yet. This site is where I put those.
